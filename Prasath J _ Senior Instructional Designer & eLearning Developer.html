About me

I am an instructional designer and eLearning developer based in Chennai. Today I develop corporate eLearning at Ramboll India with Articulate Storyline 360, Rise 360 and Vyond.

I started in Civil Engineering, so I work comfortably with subject matter experts and can turn engineering content into storyboards, scenarios and microlearning. I have led teams of designers and SMEs, delivered projects on time, and built courses with universal design in mind: clear heading structure, strong colour contrast, meaningful link text and alt text. Every course is tested before delivery and handed over with editable source files and documentation.

How I work

Every course moves through the same stages, so clients know what to expect.

  1. UnderstandClarify the audience, the goal and what learners must be able to do.
  2. StoryboardChoose an approach (scenario, case study, problem-based) and script every screen.
  3. BuildDevelop in Rise 360 and Storyline with accessible design and brand-consistent media.
  4. Test and hand overCheck function, content and accessibility, publish to the LMS and share source files.
